Circular No. 7/2026 — Revised Office Timings

With effect from 1 November 2026, all offices of the Department will function from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM on working days, with a lunch interval from 1:30 PM to 2:00 PM. Staff are requested to mark attendance through the biometric system on arrival and departure. Officers in charge of sections shall ensure that the public counter remains open throughout office hours. The annexure attached to this circular reproduces the approval of the competent authority.
