District Record Room — Annual Report 2025-26

The District Record Room handled 18,420 certified-copy applications during the year, an increase of twelve per cent over the previous year. Online applications accounted for sixty-one per cent of the total, and the average time to issue a certified copy fell from nine working days to six.

Digitisation of land records reached 84 per cent of the division's 212 villages. The remaining villages are scheduled for completion by March 2027, subject to the availability of scanning staff at the tehsil offices.
